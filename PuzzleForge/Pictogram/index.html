How to play
  • Computer: left-click a square to fill it, right-click to mark it as empty (shown as an X or a dot)
  • Phone or tablet: tap a square to cycle it — empty, then filled, then marked empty, then back to empty. There is no long-press
  • Click or tap a row/column number to gray it out once you've used it, as a reminder
  • Fill the squares so each row and column matches its numbers, with at least one gap between groups
  • Dragging across squares (mouse or finger) applies the same change to a whole line of squares at once
  • The keyboard shortcuts below need a physical keyboard, so they aren't available on most phones and tablets
  • Ctrl+Z / Left arrow — undo, Ctrl+Y / Right arrow — redo
  • + / Up arrow — zoom in, − / Down arrow — zoom out
  • Shift+S — save a point, Shift+L — load your last saved point
  • Shift+R — reset this puzzle's progress and timer (cannot be undone)
  • The timer starts on your first filled square and pauses when this tab isn't focused
  • Progress, your save point and the timer are kept on this device between visits
