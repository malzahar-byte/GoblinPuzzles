How to play (first draft)
  • Click the gap between two neighbouring islands to cycle: no bridge, one bridge, two bridges.
  • Each island's number is how many bridges touch it. Bridges are straight and never cross.
  • All islands must end up connected. A filled island has all its bridges.
  • Zoom with the mouse wheel or +/−; undo and redo with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.
